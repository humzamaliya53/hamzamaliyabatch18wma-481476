Daal Chawal Recipe

  1. Wash and soak lentils (daal) for 15-20 minutes
  2. Boil rice in salted water until tender
  3. Cook lentils with turmeric, salt, and spices
  4. Prepare tarka with onions, garlic, and cumin
  5. Pour the tarka over the daal and serve with rice
